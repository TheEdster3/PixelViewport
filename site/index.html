One display contract.
Many acquisition stacks.

01

Explicit frame lifetime

Wrap a camera-owned buffer with a lease or make a safe pooled copy. Replaced and displayed frames are disposed deterministically.

02

Live-stream backpressure

A single-slot mailbox ensures a busy UI presents the newest available frame rather than replaying an obsolete queue.

03

Imaging-native interaction

Cursor-centered zoom, constrained pan, raw pixel inspection, and image-coordinate overlays stay consistent across adapters.

04

Measurable by design
